Decay is physics; uptake is biology.
  • The calculation removes the radioactive decay exactly, whatever the uptake time.
  • It cannot remove the biology: FDG keeps building up in most tumours after an hour, so a later scan usually gives a higher SUV.
  • That is why the EANM guideline fixes the uptake time: 60 min, acceptable 55–75 min. A repeat scan should match the first uptake time to within 10 min (Boellaard 2015). This patient: 55 min.
  • Even with everything matched, SUVmax repeats only to within about 12 % (same scanner). QIBA counts a change as real only from +39 % or −28 % (tumours of 2 cm or more, SUVmax of 4 or more).
Same scan, same tumour: the SUV moves with every number in the header. Before you compare two SUVs, check weight, dose, clocks and uptake time on both scans.
