Task 4: has the tumour responded?

Answer first.
Nothing happened.
  • This man was never treated.
  • He was in a repeatability study: 15 men, each scanned twice within 2 weeks (Fedorov et al., 2017).
  • Every difference comes from the measurement: positioning, coil, noise, ROI.
Orange: this man. Red lines: limits, not results.

All the men with a tumour in the same study

When is a change real?
  • Only when it is bigger than the limit: 47 % for prostate (QIBA), 42 % in this study.
  • The limit is not a count of patients. A repeated scan stays inside it 19 times out of 20.
  • So 1 in 20 lands outside by chance. Here 1 of 10 did: a tiny lesion, +44 %.
  • Average change with no treatment: 16 %. This man's is ordinary.
