The radiologist did the same.
  • They found the tumour with T2w, ADC and DCE side by side, then drew it on each series separately, the DWI outline on the ADC map (Fedorov et al., Sci Data 2018).
  • Their ADC outline sits about 3 mm further back than their T2w outline (5 mm in all, partly towards the feet). ADC is calculated from the b 0 and b 1400 images, so b 1400 is shifted the same way.
  • The DWI is distorted: the air-filled balloon in the rectum bends the magnetic field, and DWI (EPI) turns that into a shift along the phase-encoding direction (front–back here).
  • The DWI was also taken about 10 minutes after the T2w. The patient can move in between.
  • In the study, tumour volumes drawn on ADC repeated worse than on T2w: 112 % vs 71 % (Fedorov et al., 2017).

Discuss

DWI shows where the tumour is. T2w (or CT) shows where it is in space. Contour on the geometrically correct image and use DWI as a guide, unless you have measured the shift and it is small.
